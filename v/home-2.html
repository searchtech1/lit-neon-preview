How it works

Approve the drawing. We make exactly that.

  1. Design it

    Type your words, pick the lettering, colour and size. The price moves as you go, and nothing is hidden until checkout.

  2. Approve the drawing

    Within 12 hours you get a proof drawing like the one here: exact words, letter height, overall size. Tweaks are free.

  3. We make it

    Your tube is bent, mounted on acrylic and tested in our UK workshop.

  4. Hang it

    Screws and standoffs, chains, adhesive strips or a stand. It plugs into a normal socket.

Good to know

The questions people ask first

How does it hang?
You choose in the designer: wall screws with standoffs (the classic, for plaster or brick), hanging chains, adhesive strips (small, light signs on smooth walls, handy for renters) or a table stand for shelves and bars. It plugs into a normal socket, so there's no wiring to do.
How does it arrive, and when?
Boxed with the power supply, the dimmer and remote, and your fixings. It arrives 7–10 working days after you approve your proof drawing; weekends and UK bank holidays don't count. The designer and your basket show the dates for your postcode.
What does it cost to run?
LED neon runs on a low-voltage supply and stays cool to the touch, so it's happy on a bedroom wall all evening. The workshop is measuring the real figure per metre of tube; we'll print the number once it's been measured, not guessed.
What does the warranty cover?
Two years on the LEDs, the power supply, the dimmer and the remote, under normal indoor use. It doesn't cover accidental damage, or a standard indoor sign used outdoors. Your statutory rights are not affected.
Can I return it?
Custom signs are made to your words, so they can't be returned for a change of mind once we start making them. You can cancel free of charge any time before you approve your proof drawing, and if anything is faulty we'll repair or remake it. Ready-made designs can be returned within fourteen days of delivery.
Can it go outside?
Standard signs are for indoors. For a covered porch, a shop front or a garden bar we quote an outdoor build with a sealed supply, so pick “Outdoors” in the designer or tell us when you send a logo.
