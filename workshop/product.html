What's in the box

Your sign on its acrylic backing, a 12 V adapter with a UK 3-pin plug, a dimmer and remote, 2 m of clear cable and wall fixings. Other plugs are available in the builder.

Delivery

Free tracked delivery to any UK address. Standard making time is 5 working days, then delivery. The arrival window above skips weekends and bank holidays. Times are placeholders until our supplier confirms them.

Returns

Ready-made designs are still made to order in your chosen colour and size, so they can't be returned for a change of mind. If a sign arrives damaged or faulty, tell us within 30 days and we'll replace or repair it.

Installation

Cut-to-shape signs hang from pre-drilled holes with the fixings supplied. Rectangle signs mount on four polished standoffs. You need a drill, a spirit level and ten minutes.
