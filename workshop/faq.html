Ordering and proofs

What happens after I order?

We email you a drawing of your sign with every dimension marked, usually the next working day. You check the spelling, size and colour and reply to approve it, or ask for changes. Making starts only after your yes.

Can I change my mind after approving the drawing?

Before approval, change anything as often as you like. After approval the sign is cut and bent to that drawing, so changes aren't possible. Contact us straight away if you spot something and we'll tell you honestly where the sign has got to.

How do prices work?

Price depends on the overall size of the sign, which comes from your words, the lettering and the letter height you pick. Backing, finish, outdoor use and express making add fixed amounts. Everything shown includes VAT. Prices are placeholders until our supplier costs are confirmed.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, Klarna Pay in 3 is offered at checkout: three interest-free payments, the first taken when you order. Klarna runs its own eligibility check.

Can I see it on my own wall first?

Yes. In the builder, choose "Your wall" and pick a photo. It stays on your device and is never uploaded. The room view also shows the sign at true scale against a sofa, so you can judge the size.

02

Delivery

How much is delivery?

Free to any UK address, tracked. We only ship to the UK at the moment.

When will it arrive?

Standard making time is 5 working days, express is 2, then one to two working days in transit. The builder and product pages show an arrival window that skips weekends and UK bank holidays. Order before 2pm for the time to start that day. Making times are placeholders until our supplier confirms them.

Do I need to be in?

Signs are tracked and need a signature because of the size. You'll get the courier's tracking link by email and can usually rearrange the day.

How is it packed?

Flat, in a box with foam around the acrylic, with the adapter, remote, cable and fixings in a separate bag inside. Keep the box until the sign is on the wall and working.

03

Returns

Can I return a custom sign?

Custom and personalised goods are made to your specification, so they aren't covered by the usual 14-day change-of-mind right under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations. That is exactly why we send a drawing to approve before making anything.

What if it arrives damaged or faulty?

Tell us within 30 days with a photo and we'll repair or replace it at no cost, including collection. Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 apply to any fault.

What if the sign doesn't match the drawing?

Then we've made a mistake and we put it right. Compare it with the drawing you approved and send us a photo.

Are ready-made designs returnable?

Ready-made designs are still made to order in your colour and size, so the same rules apply as for custom signs.

04

Installation

How do I hang a cut-to-shape sign?

It has pre-drilled holes along the top edge. Use the supplied screws and wall plugs for solid walls, or clear line and hooks for a backdrop. Mark the holes with the drawing as a guide, check with a spirit level, then hang.

How do standoffs work?

Rectangle signs come with four polished standoffs. Drill four holes, fix the barrels to the wall, sit the panel on them and screw the caps on. The sign floats about 2 cm off the wall.

Can it sit on a shelf instead?

Yes. Choose the table stand in the builder and the sign comes with an acrylic base so it stands on a shelf, counter or mantelpiece.

How heavy is it?

Acrylic and LED flex are light; most signs under a metre wide weigh less than a framed picture of the same size. We'll give the weight of your sign on the drawing once supplier figures are confirmed.

05

Power and plug

What plug does it come with?

A UK 3-pin plug on a 12 V adapter as standard. EU, US and AU plugs can be chosen in the builder if the sign is a gift abroad.

How long is the cable?

2 m of clear cable from the sign to the adapter, then the adapter's own lead to the socket. An extension lead is fine.

How do I dim it or turn it off?

With the remote, or the inline dimmer on the cable. Many people plug it into a smart plug so it comes on with the evening lights.

How much electricity does it use?

Low-voltage LED flex uses a small fraction of what glass neon or a lamp with a filament bulb would. We'll publish the figure for each size once we have the measured draw of the components we ship, rather than guess.

06

Outdoor use

Can the sign go outside?

Choose "Outdoors" in the builder for a weatherproof version with sealed connections. Standard signs are for indoor use only.

What about the adapter?

The plug-in adapter is for indoor use whatever sign you choose. Run the cable inside, or house the adapter in a dry, covered enclosure.

Porches, marquees and gardens?

All fine with the outdoor option. Direct sun all day will fade acrylic over years, so a covered spot keeps it looking new for longer.

07

Care

How do I clean it?

Unplug it and wipe with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth. No sprays, solvents or glass cleaner on the acrylic, which can cloud it.

Can I leave it on all the time?

You can, but the remote has a timer and most people switch it off overnight. Dimmed, it runs cooler and lasts longer.

What if a section stops lighting?

Contact us with a photo. Within 30 days we repair or replace it free; after that we'll quote for a repair honestly. We'll publish a warranty period once our supplier terms are confirmed rather than promise one we can't back yet.
