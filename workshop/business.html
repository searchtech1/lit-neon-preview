How a logo job runs

Like a signwriter's brief: you give us the artwork and the wall, we come back with a drawing you can measure against it.

What we need from you

  1. 1Your logo or letteringSVG, PDF or AI if you have it; a sharp PNG works too.
  2. 2Roughly how wide it should beOr the wall width and we'll suggest a size.
  3. 3Where it hangsIndoors, in a window, or somewhere it meets weather.
  4. 4Colours you'd likeAny of our tube colours, or the brand colour to match as closely as we can.

What you get back

  1. 1A drawing with tube paths and dimensionsSome logo details become single lines or are simplified; we show you exactly where.
  2. 2A price, inc. VAT, with deliveryFixed once you approve. Changes before approval are free.
  3. 3Your approval before we startNothing is made until you say yes to the drawing.
