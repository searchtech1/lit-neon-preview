Wedding questions

Can we hire a sign for the day?

No. Every sign is made to order and yours to keep. It costs about the same as a weekend's hire from the companies that do, and you still have it afterwards.

How do we hang it at the venue?

Cut-to-shape signs have pre-drilled holes for clear fishing line, hooks or cable ties, so they hang from flower walls, arches and drapes. Rectangle signs mount on standoffs or sit on the table stand. Ask the venue where the nearest socket is; the cable is 2 m long and an extension lead is fine.

Can it go outside for a marquee or garden?

Choose outdoors in the builder for a weatherproof sign. The plug-in adapter must stay indoors or in a dry, covered spot.

Can you match our colour scheme?

We have twelve tube colours, including colour-changing. Pick the closest in the builder and we show it on a wall colour of your choice, or send a photo of your own wall.

Can we have two lines, or a date?

Yes. Up to three lines of 24 characters, so "Emma & James" over "12 June 2027" works. Longer lines mean a wider sign; the builder shows the exact width as you type.
