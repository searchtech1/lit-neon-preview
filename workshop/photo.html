Photo to neon

How it works

1

Add a photo

Choose one from your device, or use the live camera. Faces in profile, pets, buildings and simple logos trace best.

2

Tune the drawing

Slide the detail and tube thickness until the outlines read cleanly, then pick a colour and switch the lights off to see the unlit tubes.

3

Download or get a quote

Save the PNG, or send us the render and we'll redraw it properly, with every dimension marked, and price it. You approve before anything is made.

What traces well

  • Clear subject against a plain or blurred background
  • Side-on portraits and profiles
  • Pets with a visible outline, ears and tail
  • Buildings, bikes, bottles and other simple silhouettes
  • Flat logos and line art

What struggles

  • Busy backgrounds and crowds
  • Very fine detail like fur texture or small text
  • Low-light photos with soft edges
  • Gradients and shading, which neon can't show

A real sign is one continuous tube per shape, so we simplify traces by hand before quoting.
