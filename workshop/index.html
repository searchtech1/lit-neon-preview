Good to know

How long does a sign take?

Standard making time is 5 working days, with express at 2. Delivery is on top, and you see the arrival window on the sign before you pay. Times are placeholders until our supplier confirms them.

Can I return a custom sign?

Custom signs are made to your words, so they can't be returned for a change of mind. That is why we send a drawing to approve first. If a sign arrives damaged or faulty, we put it right.

Does it come with a plug?

Yes. Every sign ships with a UK 3-pin plug and a low-voltage adapter as standard, plus a dimmer and remote. EU, US and AU plugs are an option in the builder.
