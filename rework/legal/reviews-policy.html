How we handle reviews

We don’t have reviews yet, because we’ve only just started. When they come, this is how we’ll handle them.

      How reviews are collected

      After your sign is delivered, we may invite you to review it through Judge.me (for product reviews on this site) and Trustpilot. We invite every customer whose order has been delivered, not just the ones we think are happy.

      Our promise

      • No paid reviews. We never pay for reviews or offer discounts, gifts or entry into prize draws in exchange for them.
      • No filtering. We publish every genuine review, positive or negative, as long as it is about the product or our service and isn’t abusive or unlawful.
      • No editing. We never change the words or the rating of a review. We may reply to it, publicly, under our own name.
      • No fake reviews. No reviews are written by us, our staff or anyone connected to us, and we don’t buy or swap reviews.

      Negative reviews

      If something has gone wrong, we’d rather know. We’ll reply to a negative review to put things right, and we won’t ask anyone to remove or change a review as a condition of a refund or a fix.

      When a review may be removed

      We, or the review platform, may remove a review only if it contains personal information about someone else, is abusive or discriminatory, isn’t about a genuine experience with us, or breaks the platform’s own rules. We keep a record of any removal and the reason.

      Why this matters

      Fake and selectively published reviews mislead people. UK consumer law, including the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, bans them, and we agree with it. If you think a review about us breaks these rules, tell us through our contact form.
