Cookies and browser storage

Your design, your basket and your choices live in your own browser. Essential storage keeps the site working; analytics is off unless you switch it on here.

Last updated 10 Oct 2026Draft for the owner: not yet reviewed by a lawyer

On this page

    On this page

      Your settings

      Changes take effect as soon as you save. You can come back and change them whenever you like.

      Essential

      Keeps your design, your basket, your delivery postcode and this choice. Without it the designer and checkout can’t work, so it can’t be switched off.

      Always on

      Analytics

      Counts which pages and buttons are used, such as “opened the designer” or “added to basket”, so we can see what helps. No names, no emails, nothing sold.

      Marketing

      We don’t use marketing cookies today. If we ever add them, for example to measure an advert, they’ll only run if this is on.

      Exactly what we store

      We don’t load third-party tracking scripts. Here is everything this site keeps in your browser:

      Clearing it

      You can clear everything at any time from your browser’s settings (look for “site data” or “cookies and site data”). Clearing it removes your saved design and basket too, so copy a share link from the designer first if you want to keep a design.
